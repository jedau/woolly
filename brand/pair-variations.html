The pair, overlapped into a W

McDonald’s arches are two curves that overlap into a letter. Hurley’s brackets never cross, so they stay two marks. These keep the two tusks and cross them once, so the pair itself is the W.

Pair now

Two tusks, side by side, no cross.

Hurley

Two brackets. They do not cross.

Golden Arches

Two curves overlap into one letter.

Overlap

Two tusks, each bent through the middle and laid over its pair. Where the arches cross into an M, these cross into a W.
